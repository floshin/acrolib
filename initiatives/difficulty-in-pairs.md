# A suggestion in regard to difficulty in pair motions

We have a situation now in which many pairs are performing easier elements than they could and don't risk anything, because it wouldn't be rewarding, since the execution is valued so much more than the difficulty. 

The rules have been changed accordingly for a reason. They favour and promote cleaner routines and most importantly: It is a big step to reduce the injury risk, especially in dynamics, but also in high-risk pyramids in Groups. 

It totally makes sense to keep these rules intact to reduce the injury risk. But for Pairs, where there is very little injury risk in Balance, it created a situation where they do not e.g. perform a Diamidov anymore just because it could wobble a bit and you could see the struggle, while there isn't much injury risk involved. Seeing some struggle in Balance in Pairs is thrilling and very exciting to watch for the audience. Only if the elements are rewarded enough would we also see higher difficulty elements and motions in Pairs again. That means that at least in regard to those elements which have very little injury risk, mostly related to Balance, we should encourage higher difficulty and reward it more again.
