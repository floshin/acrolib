# Corrections in Skill Training

When coaching acrobatics and during skill training, coaches are using different cues in order to correct the athlete, whenever there is any technical mistake. 

Such technical mistakes or skill issues can be corrected: 
* with or without physical support.
* by the coach or by the athletes themselves.

Physical support can be more or less and should be striven to become contineously less until there is none anymore. And all mistakes should eventually be possible to be corrected by the athletes themselves; otherwise there would be no control over the element. 

That said, in the beginning there may arise a need for much physical support, for the coach to correct mistakes by his own hands on the athlete. He should offer such hands-on support as much as necessary, but at the same time he should try to avoid it and to diminish it as much as possible. The less physical support is needed, the better. 

In order to achieve this, the coach...  
(1) first has to make sure, that the athlete is physically capable of the movement (in regard to strength, flexibility and control)  
(2) then he has to make sure that the athlete can do the skill and correct it on his own, that he is able to get into the correct position, move correctly, as soon as a verbal cue is given that relates to the particular mistake made.
