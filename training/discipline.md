# Discipline in Training

flosha, 09.05.2026

Apart from motivation discipline is another important factor in training. At first we have to define discipline.  

Discipline means the constant readyness to do what is necessary. It does not mean blind obedience to what the coach says, even if it may sometimes seem so. It means doing what is necessary in order to achieve ones goals. If the goals of the coach and the goals of the athletes are the same and in harmony, and when the means that the coach chooses to achieve these goals are clear to and understood by the athlete and agreed upon, then it has nothing to do with blind obedience.  

Thus, all the points addressed in our article on [motivation](/training/motivation) are at the same time serving discipline in a general way, in the sense of the readyness memtioned above, but not so much in a sense of an actual application of discipline. For this there are other important factors, and these have to do with the organisation of the training. 

With organisation of training we do not mean programming or planning, it is not primarily about what exactly is done, but it is about how what has to be done is done, how constantly it is done, how this constant practice is ensured, how this motivation or work ethic can be created or maintained...
